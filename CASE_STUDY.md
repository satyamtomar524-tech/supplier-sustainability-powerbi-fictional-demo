# From college project to an evidence-review decision

## Historical context

The 2025 ISM project was team coursework. The recovery guide reports Satyam's contribution to data preparation in R/Excel, criteria, team Power BI work and presentation. It records a BMW FIZ presentation on 11 December 2025; that historical date is carried as reported, not freshly authenticated from email during this rebuild. The scenario is not a reconstruction of BMW's actual supplier results and carries no endorsement.

October 2026 AI-assisted reconstruction and the November 2026 report-definition audit are subsequent work. The audit found row-versus-label counting problems, inconsistent aggregations and unsupported certification interpretations. Its static evidence boundary remains intact: it did not independently decode every saved model object or execute the original DAX.

## Decision and method

The new question is: what needs reviewing next? Start with candidate labels and source rows, then review evidence with explicit scope and unit. An identity/scope blocker is P1. Missing, old or unreviewed information is P2. A record with usable example evidence is P3. Keep the reason and requested action beside the record.

Eight workshops cover each branch of this authored rule. Eleven raw cells include whitespace, repeated labels and a numeric placeholder. Nine evidence rows include one exact duplicate. The reproducible R workflow yields eight labels, ten occurrences and eight claims. Two labels have usable evidence and six need follow-up.

## What changed in the report

The earlier three-label release was a filter fixture. This rebuild adds meaningful units and reporting years, a documented priority queue, source/scope inspection, explicit next actions, reproducible public inputs and expected-result tests. It makes the original data-quality lesson visible in an analytical workflow.

## Practical interpretation

Workshop C's 900 MWh value stays visible but cannot be attributed confidently until identity is resolved. Workshop E's 1,800 water-withdrawal value cannot be interpreted without a unit. These gaps are tasks for a reviewer, not evidence of poor supplier sustainability.

A's invented 20% within-site energy change is comparable across its own two periods. It cannot be compared directly with B's group energy intensity. Scope and denominator matter before arithmetic.
