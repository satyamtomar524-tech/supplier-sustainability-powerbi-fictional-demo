## 0:00-0:35 - context and question

This began as an ISM college team project in 2025. The later recovery is separate work, with substantial Codex assistance. The original internal records stay private. This public report uses eight invented workshops to answer a practical question: which evidence records need reviewing next, and why?

## 0:35-1:10 - cleaning and definitions

Eleven supplier cells become ten occurrences and eight labels. A repeated workshop remains in the source log but counts once in the label population. A numeric zero is retained in the exclusion log. Nine example evidence rows become eight claims after one exact duplicate is removed. A supplier label is not a verified legal company.

## 1:10-1:50 - demonstrate the decision

On Review Overview, select Machining. There are two labels, both reviewed, with one usable-evidence case and one follow-up. Clear the filter and the baseline returns to eight labels, 75% reviewed, two usable and six follow-ups. On Supplier Evidence, choose Workshop C: identity must be resolved before its energy figure can be attributed. Choose E: its water value lacks a unit. These are evidence tasks, not poor-performance scores.

## 1:50-2:30 - explain a technical correction

For Machining, a global numerator would count all six reviewed labels over two selected labels and incorrectly show 300%. The correct numerator counts the same selected population: two divided by two, or 100%. This is a tested fictional example of the filter-context problem. Separately, the rebuild script now supports native Desktop-saved column names and validates every output before replacing files.

## 2:30-3:00 - interpretation and limits

Workshop A's invented site energy use falls from 1,500 to 1,200 MWh, a 20% within-site change. Workshop B reports group-level intensity, so the two cannot be ranked together. The report uses reporting years 2023-2025; its fictional snapshot date and actual UTC refresh timestamp have different meanings. It proves no BMW savings, verified certifications or real supplier ranking. Original historical reconciliation remains separate.
